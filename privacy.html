Privacy Policy

Expense Monitor & Invoices · Quantum TS Labs

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Expense Monitor is built to keep your money data on your own phone. This policy explains the few cases where anything leaves it, and what you can do about each one.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Everything you type — expenses, income, subscriptions, budgets, categories, loans, invoices, scanned documents and receipt photos — is stored only on your device. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server belonging to us. We cannot see your data, and we do not have a copy of it.

ADVERTISING

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob reads your device's advertising ID and may use it, together with technical information such as your device model, coarse location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with an ad, to select and measure ads. This information is collected and shared by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings, under Settings > Google > Ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change that answer at any time from Settings > Ad privacy options inside the app.

OPTIONAL GOOGLE DRIVE BACKUP

If — and only if — you sign in and switch backup on, the app copies your data to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. That copy can include your financial records, the photos you attached to entries, and any names, email addresses or company details you typed into an invoice.

That folder is a private application folder in your Drive. Only this app can open it. We have no access to it, and the data does not pass through any server of ours.

To remove it: sign out in the app, then delete the app's data from Google Drive (drive.google.com > Settings > Manage apps > Expense Monitor > Delete hidden app data). Uninstalling the app alone does not delete the Drive copy.

NETWORK REQUESTS THAT CARRY NO PERSONAL DATA

  1. Exchange rates. When rates are refreshed, the app requests public currency rates from frankfurter.app. Nothing about you is sent.
  2. App settings. The app fetches a small configuration file from GitHub so features and ad settings can be adjusted without an update. It is a plain download; nothing about you is sent.

The document scanner runs entirely on your device. Scanned pages and receipt photos are not uploaded anywhere unless you turn on the Drive backup above.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO

There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling of anyone's data. We do not build a profile of you, because we never receive your data in the first place.

CHILDREN

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

DELETING YOUR DATA

Uninstalling removes everything stored on the device. If you used the Drive backup, delete it separately using the steps above. Before uninstalling, use Export to keep your own copy — we cannot recover anything for you.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
