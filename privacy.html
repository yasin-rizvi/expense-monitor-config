Privacy Policy

Expense Monitor & Invoices · Quantum TS Labs

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Expense Monitor is designed to keep your financial records under your control. This policy explains what stays on your device, the optional services you can choose to use, and how you can delete your data.

DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Your expenses, income, subscriptions, budgets, categories, loans, invoices, scanned documents and receipt photos are stored in the app's private storage on your device. No Expense Monitor account is required. Google sign-in is used only if you choose the optional Google Drive backup described below.

We do not operate a server that receives or stores your financial records, and we cannot view or recover those records.

Depending on your Android and Google backup settings, Android may include app data in a Google-managed device backup or device-to-device transfer. This is an Android system service controlled through your device's backup settings, not a server operated by us.

ADVERTISING

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To provide and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process your device's advertising ID, device information, coarse location derived from your IP address, and interactions with ads. Google handles this information under its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings under Settings > Google > Ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for consent before requesting an ad, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can review your choices from Settings > Ad privacy options inside the app.

OPTIONAL GOOGLE PLAY PURCHASE

The app may offer a one-time Google Play purchase to remove ads. Google Play processes the transaction. The app receives the purchase status needed to unlock the feature, but we do not receive your card number, bank details or other payment credentials. Google's privacy policy applies to information processed by Google Play.

OPTIONAL GOOGLE DRIVE BACKUP

If — and only if — you choose Google Drive backup, the app asks you to sign in with Google and authorize the drive.appdata permission. This permission lets the app create, read, update and delete its own hidden backup files in your Google Drive application-data folder. It does not let the app read your other Drive files.

A backup can contain your financial records, subscriptions, budgets, invoices, scanned documents, receipt photos, and any names, email addresses or company details you entered. The backup is used only to back up, restore and synchronize the app features you request. We do not use Google Drive data for advertising, analytics, credit decisions, sale to data brokers, or training artificial-intelligence models.

The hidden backup folder is in your own Google Drive account and is protected by Google's account and storage security. Data is transferred to Google over encrypted HTTPS connections. We have no server-side access to your Drive backup, and the data does not pass through a server operated by us.

The backup remains in your Google Drive until you delete it. To remove it, sign out in the app and then go to Google Drive > Settings > Manage apps > Expense Monitor > Delete hidden app data. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete the Drive copy.

OTHER NETWORK REQUESTS

  1. Exchange rates. When you refresh rates, the app requests public currency rates from frankfurter.app. The request does not include your financial records.
  2. App configuration. The app downloads a small configuration file from GitHub so features, notices and ad settings can be adjusted without an app update. The request does not include your financial records.

The document scanner runs on your device. Scanned pages and receipt photos are not uploaded by the scanner; they leave the device only if you choose Drive backup or explicitly share/export them.

SECURITY

Local records are kept in Android's private app storage. You may also enable the app lock, which uses Android's biometric or device-credential prompt. The app does not receive or store your fingerprint or face data. Network connections used by the app are encrypted with HTTPS. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO

There is no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no sale of personal data, and no use of your Google Drive data for advertising or tracking across other apps or websites. AdMob's advertising data practices are described separately above.

CHILDREN

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

RETENTION AND DELETION

Records remain in the app until you delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Android system backup may retain or restore app data according to your device and Google backup settings; you can manage that backup from Android settings. If you used Google Drive backup, delete the hidden Drive data separately using the steps above. Before deleting the app, use Export if you want to keep your own copy. We cannot recover deleted records for you.

Google Play retains purchase records under Google's policies. Removing the app does not delete your Google Play purchase history.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. The current version is always available at this URL.
