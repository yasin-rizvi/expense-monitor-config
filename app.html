Expense Monitor & Invoices

Organize everyday expenses, track budgets and subscriptions, create invoices, and keep receipt documents together.

Your records, your choice

Core expense, budget, invoice and receipt features work without creating an app account or signing in. Records are stored on your device. Invoice client details are entered by you; the app does not automatically read your phone contacts.

Optional Google Drive backup

You can choose to sign in with your existing Google account to back up and restore app records and receipt attachments. This uses the app's private data folder in your own Google Drive, not access to your ordinary Drive files. Google sign-in is optional, and no developer-hosted user account is created.

Privacy and support

Read our Privacy Policy for details about local storage, optional backup, Android system backup, advertising and Google Play purchases.
